Wobble Waiter Privacy Policy

Wobble Waiter: Tray Run

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short

Wobble Waiter has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your progress stays on your phone.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads, and that data contains nothing from your game.

What stays on your phone

Everything Wobble Waiter remembers is kept in small files in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your progress: your best score and distance, your tips, the trays you own and the one you use, your missions, the days you have played and today's Daily Run best
  • Settings: music, sounds, vibration, and whether the steering or forward/back tilt is flipped
  • Small things the game needs: how many runs you have played and how many since the last ad, so the ad rules below work

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. The game makes no network request of its own, and plays without a network.

Motion sensor. You steer by tilting your phone, so the game reads the phone's motion sensor (accelerometer) while you play. The readings are used on the spot to tilt the tray and are never stored or sent anywhere.

No cloud backup. Wobble Waiter does not take part in Android's backup, so your progress is not copied to your Google account. If you uninstall the app or move to a new phone, your progress starts again.

Advertising

Wobble Waiter is free and is paid for by ads. They never appear during a run:

  • A full-screen ad between runs, when you tap Again or Menu after a run ends: never in your first five runs, at most once every third run after that, and never twice within two minutes

There are no banner ads and no rewarded ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks only for ads suitable for a general audience (rated G), and passes nothing about your game to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later: tap the settings cog on the title screen, then "Privacy choices".

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

Wobble Waiter does not ask for camera, storage, photos, location, contacts, microphone, notifications, phone or SMS permission. Reading the motion sensor needs no permission on Android.

Purchases

Wobble Waiter has no purchases. Tips and trays are earned in the game, never sold.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Children

Wobble Waiter is made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to those rated for a general audience.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: contact@futigo.io.
