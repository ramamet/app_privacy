HeatTreat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

HeatTreat is a steel heat treatment calculator and reference. It computes critical temperatures, hardness, hardenability, tempering, weld preheat and carburizing estimates from published correlations, entirely on your device. It has no account and no server of ours. HeatTreat is free, and it pays for itself with an occasional full-screen ad served by Google AdMob. This policy explains, plainly, what that means.

The short version

  • Every calculation runs on your phone. What you type into HeatTreat never leaves it.
  • There is no account, no sign-up and no analytics.
  • We never receive anything about you.
  • The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads, and it contains nothing you did in the app beyond seeing an ad.

What the app stores, and where

HeatTreat keeps three things in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your temperature unit: whether you prefer Celsius or Fahrenheit
  • When the app was first opened: so that no ad appears in your first two minutes with it
  • Your ad consent choice, where the law asks for one: stored by Google's consent tool so you are not asked again each time

The composition you type into the calculator, the grade you look up, and the figures you enter are held in memory only while you are using the screen. They are not saved to disk, not kept in a history, and not sent anywhere, to us, to Google or to anyone else. Close the app and they are gone.

The grade database, all equations, and all citations are bundled inside the app. Every calculation works with no connection at all.

Advertising

HeatTreat shows an occasional full-screen ad at one natural break: when you come back from a set of results you have been reading for at least ten seconds. There is never an ad while you enter a composition or read results, when the app opens, or in your first two minutes with the app. Ads are at least three minutes apart, with no more than four each time you use the app.

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks for ads suitable for teens and adults only (rated PG at most), and passes nothing about your steels, your calculations or your use of the app to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions: for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later on the Reference tab, under "Privacy options".

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

Permissions

HeatTreat asks you for no permissions. It does not ask for camera, photos, storage, microphone, location, contacts, or notifications. Its manifest declares:

  • Internet and network state: used only by the ads SDK. The app makes no network request of its own.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads SDK, as above.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: added by Google's ads library for its own background tasks.

Estimates, not specifications

HeatTreat gives engineering estimates from published empirical correlations. It is not a certification tool and does not issue specifications. Correlations carry scatter, and your steel, your furnace and your quench are not the ones the equations were fitted on. Every result in the app shows its equation, its source, and the range over which that source is reasonable, so you can judge it yourself. Verify against the applicable standard, your supplier's data, and your own test coupons before committing work.

Children

HeatTreat is made for adults and older students working with steel: knife makers, blacksmiths, heat treaters, students and engineers. It is not directed at children under 13, and the app itself collects no personal information from anyone.
