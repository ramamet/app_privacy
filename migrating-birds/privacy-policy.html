Migrating Birds Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short

Migrating Birds has no account, no sign-up and no server of ours. Your game is saved on your phone. We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's services use: the advertising SDK in the free version, and Google Play when you buy or restore Pro.

What stays on your phone

Everything the game remembers is kept in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your levels, stars, seed and daily streak
  • The birds you have met and your aviary
  • Your settings for sound, haptics, Night Aviary and bird letters
  • How many rewarded videos you watched today, so the daily limit works
  • Pro: a yes-or-no note that Pro is unlocked

Your phone's own backup

Android's built-in backup may include these files, so your game can come back on a new phone with your Google account. This backup is made by your phone and stored in your own Google account; it is not a server of ours. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Advertising in the free version

The free version shows an occasional full-screen ad after a win screen closes, from level 6 onward. Ads are at least three minutes apart, with no more than four each time you use the app, and there is never an ad during a level. You can also choose to watch a short video for seed when you run out. That choice is always yours.

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks for ads suitable for a general audience (rated G) only. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions: for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your choice through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change it in Settings, under Privacy options.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Privacy, then Ads. Migrating Birds Pro removes full-screen ads, and the app does not start the ads SDK for Pro owners.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used by ads and Google Play Billing; the app makes no network request of its own, apart from opening this page if you tap its link
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads SDK
  • Billing: to buy Pro through Google Play
  • Wake lock and foreground service: added by the ads SDK

The app does not ask for your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app keeps only a note that Pro is unlocked.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it keeps. Reset progress in Settings clears your game. We hold nothing about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

Migrating Birds is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
