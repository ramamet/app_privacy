GPS PhotoMark Privacy Policy

Timestamp camera for photo proof

Last updated: November 2026

🔒 Our Commitment

GPS PhotoMark stamps your photos with the date, time and place. Your photos and records are yours.

GPS PhotoMark has no account and no servers. Your photos, notes and records stay on your device, and we never receive them. To show the street address and the map on a stamp, the photo's coordinates are sent to Google (Android's geocoder and Google Maps); nothing else is sent with them. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, described under Advertising below; GPS PhotoMark Pro removes all ads.

📊 Data Collection

We want to be completely transparent about what is and isn't collected.

  • No sign-up, no account, no profile
  • Your photos, notes, labels, saved sites, visits, company name and logo are never sent to us and never shared with advertisers
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting of our own
  • Address and map lookups: the photo's latitude and longitude are sent to Google to turn them into a street address (Android's built-in geocoder) and a map image (Google Maps SDK). These requests carry only the coordinates and standard technical data such as your IP address; Google's privacy policy applies to them
  • Advertising (free version only): Google AdMob collects your advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (approximate location) and ad interactions to show and measure ads, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud. The ads SDK may also send Google diagnostic information about how ads load and perform. Google's privacy policy applies to this data.

📍 Location

Location is the heart of a GPS stamp, so here is exactly how it is used:

  • GPS PhotoMark uses your precise location only while the camera (or an active visit screen) is open. It never uses location in the background.
  • The location, time, accuracy, altitude and compass direction of each photo are saved with that photo on your device, drawn on the stamp, and written into the photo file (EXIF), as you would expect from a GPS camera.
  • Without a connection, photos are stamped with coordinates and a Plus Code; the address and map are looked up later, when you choose to update them.
  • Simulated (mock) locations are detected and marked on the photo; nothing about this is sent anywhere.

Anyone you share a stamped photo with can see where and when it was taken, from the stamp and from the file's metadata. An optional QR code on the stamp contains a Google Maps link to the photo's location.

💾 Data Storage

Everything you create in GPS PhotoMark is stored on your device:

  • Original photos: the untouched originals (used to re-stamp and verify) are kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot read
  • Stamped photos: saved to your phone's gallery in the Pictures/GPS PhotoMark album, like any camera photo. Other apps you allow to see your photos can see these
  • Records: each photo's time, place, address, notes, labels and proof fingerprint; saved sites; visits; and your settings, in a private on-device database
  • Map images: the small map shown on Map stamps, saved in app-private storage
  • Your brand (Pro, optional): the company name and a copy of the logo you choose

None of this is ever uploaded to a server of ours or shared with any third party, including advertisers.

Android device backup: if you have Google backup turned on for your phone, Android may include GPS PhotoMark's app data in your phone's own encrypted backup to your Google account, so it can come back on a new phone. This is controlled by you in your phone's settings, and we cannot read it.

GPS PhotoMark requests only what a GPS camera needs:

GPS PhotoMark does not access your contacts, never records audio or video, and never uses location in the background.

💳 Purchases

GPS PhotoMark is free. It offers one optional one-time in-app purchase, “GPS PhotoMark Pro,” which removes all ads and unlocks extra features. There is no subscription.

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see, receive, or store your payment details. Your Pro status is remembered on your device and, through Google Play, tied to your Google account so you can restore it after reinstalling.

📣 Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner at the bottom of the Gallery and Visit Log screens, and occasionally a full-screen ad when you leave the Gallery after sharing or exporting (never in your first session, and at most once every four hours). Ads never appear on the camera, after taking a photo, during an active visit, or when the app opens.

  • Your photos, locations and records are never shared with AdMob or any advertiser.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), GPS PhotoMark asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. If you decline, Google shows non-personalized ads. Where offered, you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rated G).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Buying GPS PhotoMark Pro removes all ads.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

📦 Sharing, Reports & Verify

  • Photos are shared only when you share them. A single shared photo gets a caption with its place, time, Plus Code and a Google Maps link.
  • Reports (long image, PDF, Excel, ZIP) are created on your device and only leave it if you choose to share or save them somewhere.
  • Verify reads the proof code from a photo you pick and checks it against the original kept on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere to verify a photo.
  • Nothing is ever sent to us or to any cloud automatically. GPS PhotoMark has no servers to send it to.

🗑️ Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • In the app: delete photos (this removes the original, the stamped copy in your gallery and its record), saved sites and visits at any time.
  • Complete removal: uninstalling GPS PhotoMark deletes all of its private data from your device. Stamped photos already in your gallery stay there until you delete them, like any other photo. To remove a copy in your phone's Google backup, manage backups in your Google account.

Because nothing you record ever reaches us, there is no data held on our servers to request deletion of. For data held by Google (ads, address and map lookups), see Google's privacy controls or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

🧾 Proof, Not a Legal Guarantee

Stamps and proof codes are tamper-evident, not tamper-proof: Verify shows when a photo or its details were changed, but GPS PhotoMark cannot guarantee that a photo will be accepted as evidence by any person or authority.

👶 Children's Privacy

GPS PhotoMark is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and the ads in the free version are not designed for or targeted at children. Ad content is limited to a G rating.
