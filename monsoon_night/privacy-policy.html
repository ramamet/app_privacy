Monsoon Night Privacy Policy

Paper Boat

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short

Monsoon Night has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your progress stays on your phone.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads, and that data contains nothing from your game.

What stays on your phone

Everything Monsoon Night remembers is kept in one small file in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your records: your best distance, score, combo and nights, the embers you have collected, and how many runs you have played
  • Settings: sound, music, the mode (Endless, Calm or Nightmare), haptics, steering sensitivity and graphics quality
  • Small things the game needs: whether you own Monsoon Night Pro, a count of runs since the last ad (so the ad rules below work), whether Calm mode has been suggested to you, and the last few scares you saw (so they do not repeat too soon)

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. The game itself makes no network request of its own, and plays without a network.

No cloud backup. Monsoon Night does not take part in Android's backup, so your progress is not copied to your Google account. If you uninstall the app or move to a new phone, your records start again.

Advertising

Monsoon Night is free, and is paid for by ads. There is never an ad while you are playing a run.

  • A banner at the bottom of the title screen and the result card
  • A full-screen ad between runs, when you leave the result card: never in your first five runs, at most once every third run after that, never twice within two minutes, and never just after the drowned hands have taken your boat
  • Reward ads, only if you choose them: when your candle goes out you may watch one for one more match, and on the result card you may watch one to double your embers. Saying no costs nothing

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks only for ads suitable for teenagers and older (rated Teen at most), and passes nothing about your game to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions: for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later from the title screen, under "Privacy choices".

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

Purchases

Monsoon Night is free to play. Monsoon Night Pro is one optional payment, not a subscription: it removes the banner and the ads between runs. The reward ads stay offered for you to choose, so the advertising SDK still runs, and the Advertising section above applies to Pro players too.

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or UPI details, billing address or any part of the transaction. The app keeps only a yes-or-no flag that Pro is yours, and asks Google Play again each time it starts, which is how Pro comes back after a reinstall (or use Restore purchase on the title screen). Google Play's privacy policy covers the payment itself.

Embers are collected while you play; they cannot be bought with money and have no value outside the game.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Children

Monsoon Night is a horror game made for teenagers and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and the app itself collects no personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to content rated for teenagers.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here with a new date, and a material change will be noted in the app's release notes. If Monsoon Night ever gained a feature that sent more data anywhere, such as cloud saves or leaderboards, it would be described here plainly before the feature shipped.
