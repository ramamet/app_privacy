Chinnu's Way Home Privacy Policy

Calm Maze

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In short

Chinnu's Way Home has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your progress stays on your phone.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads in Journey, and that data contains nothing from your game.

What stays on your phone

Everything Chinnu's Way Home remembers is kept in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your progress, for Journey and Little Lanes separately: the paths and lanes you have walked, your stars, the friends you have met, the kolams you have finished, your marigolds, the village houses you have restored, and your postcards
  • Settings: sound effects, evening ambience, vibration, direction buttons, reduced motion, and which mode you played last
  • A playtest log: for each maze you start, which maze it was, when you started and how long you took, your moves, hints, pauses and wrong turns, the route Chinnu walked, what you found on the way, and how it ended. It has no names or personal data, and is never sent anywhere by the app. Settings has a "Copy log" button, so a tester can choose to paste it into a message themselves, and a "Clear" button
  • One small thing the ads need: when you first opened the game, so there are no ads in your first minutes

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. The game itself makes no network request of its own, and plays without a network.

Android backup. If Android's backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include the game's progress and settings in it, so they can come back on a new phone. This is Android's own feature, under your control in your phone's settings; we never see the backup.

Advertising

Chinnu's Way Home is free, and is paid for by ads in Journey:

  • a small banner in its own strip below the maze, from your third path on;
  • now and then a full-screen ad when you tap Next path after Chinnu gets home: never in your first five paths or your first minutes, at most one every three paths and four minutes, and at most four in a session.

There is never a full-screen ad while you are walking a maze, and there are no ads at all in Little Lanes.

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks only for ads suitable for all audiences (rated G), and passes nothing about your game to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions: for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later in Settings, under "Privacy choices".

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

The gentle vibration on turns, gates and arrival needs no permission, and can be switched off in Settings. Chinnu's Way Home does not ask for camera, storage, photo library, location, contacts, microphone, notifications, phone or SMS permission.

Purchases

There are none. Chinnu's Way Home has no in-app purchases. Marigolds are picked in the lanes and spent in the game on restoring the village; they cannot be bought with money and have no value outside the game.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Children

Chinnu's Way Home is made for teenagers and adults who want a calm puzzle. It is not directed at children under 13, and the app itself collects no personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to content suitable for all audiences.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here with a new date, and a material change will be noted in the app's release notes. If Chinnu's Way Home ever gained a feature that sent more data anywhere, such as cloud saves or sharing, it would be described here plainly before the feature shipped.
