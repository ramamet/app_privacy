Claywheel Privacy Policy

Claywheel: Relaxing Pottery

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short

Claywheel has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your pots and progress stay on your phone.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads, and what Google Play handles when you make a purchase. Neither contains your pots or anything you make in the game.

What stays on your phone

Everything Claywheel remembers is kept in small files in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your shelf: the pots you choose to save, with their shape, painting and glaze
  • Your progress: your coins, the glazes and stamps you have unlocked, your best stars for each order, how many pots you have finished, and whether you have seen the first-pot hints
  • Settings: sound effects, vibration, reduce motion and graphics quality
  • Claywheel Pro: whether you own it (Google Play confirms this, see Purchases)

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. The game makes no network request of its own, and plays without a network.

No cloud backup. Claywheel does not take part in Android's backup, so your shelf and progress are not copied to your Google account. If you uninstall the app or move to a new phone, they start again. A Claywheel Pro purchase is restored by Google Play.

Advertising

Claywheel is free and is paid for by ads. No ad ever appears while you shape, paint or fire a pot, and your very first pot has no ads at all:

  • A small banner at the top of the home screen and the shelf, never on the screens where you make a pot
  • A full-screen ad when you leave a finished pot's result screen: never for your first two pots, and never twice within two minutes

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks only for ads suitable for a general audience (rated G), and passes nothing about your pots or your game to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later with "Privacy choices" in Settings on the home screen.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

Purchases

Claywheel offers one optional purchase, Claywheel Pro: no ads, every glaze, brush style and design, and an unlimited shelf. It is bought once, through Google Play.

Google Play handles the payment and your payment details; we never see them. The game only remembers on your phone that you own Pro, and asks Google Play to confirm it when the game starts, so a purchase comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone ("Restore purchase" in Settings). Purchases are covered by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored, including your shelf
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Children

Claywheel is made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to those rated for a general audience.

Changes and contact
