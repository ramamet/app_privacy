Calorie Games Privacy Policy

Guess calories from real food photos

Last updated: November 2026

🔒 Our Commitment

Calorie Games: Food Photos is a casual puzzle game. You play with photos of real meals and guess, sort and match their calories.

Calorie Games has no account and no servers. Your progress stays on your device, and we never receive it. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, described under Advertising below. Calorie Games Pro removes all ads.

📊 Data Collection

We want to be completely transparent about what is and isn't collected.

  • No sign-up, no account, no profile
  • We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting of our own
  • Your game progress is never sent to us and never shared with advertisers
  • Advertising (free version only): Google AdMob collects your advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it) and ad interactions to show and measure ads, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud. The ads SDK may also send Google diagnostic information about how ads load and perform. Google's privacy policy applies to this data.
  • Purchases: Google Play handles the optional Pro purchase, as described below.

💾 Data Storage

The little the game remembers is stored only on your device:

  • Progress: the stars you earned on each level and your best Frenzy score
  • Frenzy runs: how many free Frenzy runs you played today
  • Settings: sound effects, music and vibration on or off
  • Pro: whether you bought Calorie Games Pro, so the game works offline

None of this is ever uploaded to a server of ours or shared with any third party, including advertisers.

Android device backup: if you have Google backup turned on for your phone, Android may include the game's data in your phone's own encrypted backup to your Google account, so your progress can come back on a new phone. You control this in your phone's settings, and we cannot read it.

👑 Purchases

The one-time Calorie Games Pro purchase unlocks every level and unlimited Frenzy, and removes all ads. Google Play processes the payment, so we never see or store your payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether Pro was bought on your Google account, so you can restore it after reinstalling. See the Google Play Terms of Service.

📣 Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on the level map, and occasionally a full-screen ad between levels. Full-screen ads never appear in the first levels or in the middle of a game, and at most once every few minutes.

  • Your game progress is never shared with AdMob or any advertiser.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Calorie Games asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. If you decline, Google shows non-personalized ads. Where offered, you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rated G).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Buying Calorie Games Pro removes all ads, and the ads SDK is not started for Pro players.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

🗑️ Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • In the game: Settings → Reset progress clears your stars and best Frenzy score.
  • Complete removal: uninstalling Calorie Games deletes all of its data from your device. To remove a copy in your phone's Google backup, manage backups in your Google account.

Because nothing you do in the game reaches us, there is no data held on our servers to request deletion of. For data held by Google for ads, see Google's privacy controls or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

🥗 Food Photos & Calorie Values

The food photos and nutrition values come from the Nutrition5k dataset by Google Research, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Calorie values are for fun and learning only, and are not medical or dietary advice.

👶 Children's Privacy

Calorie Games is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and the ads in the free version are not designed for or targeted at children. Ad content is limited to a G rating.
