LienKeeper Privacy Policy

Remodel payments and lien waivers

Last updated: November 2026

🔒 Our Commitment

LienKeeper keeps the record of your remodel: what you paid, to whom, and the lien waivers that go with it. That record is yours.

LienKeeper has no account and no servers. Everything you enter stays on your device. We do not collect, store, share, or sell your projects, payments, waivers, documents or photos. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which uses device information described under Advertising below; LienKeeper Pro removes all ads.

📊 Data Collection

We want to be completely transparent about what is and isn't collected.

  • No sign-up, no account, no profile
  • Your project details, addresses, payments, contractor and supplier names, waivers, documents and photos are never collected and never shared, including with advertisers
  • No usage analytics of our own
  • Advertising (free version only): Google AdMob collects your advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (approximate location) and ad interactions to show and measure ads, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud. The ads SDK may also send Google diagnostic information about how ads load and perform. Google's privacy policy applies to this data.

💾 Data Storage

Everything you create in LienKeeper is stored locally on your device, in app-private storage that other apps cannot read:

  • Projects: Name, address, state, contract amount, milestones and your own progress estimate, stored in a private on-device database
  • Payments & waivers: Amounts, dates, payees, check numbers, cleared dates, lien waivers, preliminary notices and change orders, stored on device
  • Parties: The contractors, subcontractors and suppliers you add, with any contact details or license numbers you choose to enter
  • Photos & PDFs (optional): Copies of waivers, checks, contracts and other documents you attach, saved in app-private storage and never uploaded
  • Settings: Your reminder and display preferences, stored on device

None of this is ever uploaded to a server of ours or shared with any third party, including advertisers.

Android device backup: if you have Google backup turned on for your phone, Android may include LienKeeper's records (not your photos or PDFs) in your phone's own encrypted backup to your Google account, so they can come back on a new phone. This is controlled by you in your phone's settings, and we cannot read it.

📱 Permissions

LienKeeper requests only the minimum permissions needed, and the core ledger works without any of them:

Camera Take a photo of a waiver, check or document. LienKeeper opens your phone's own camera app and only receives the photo you take, so it requests no camera permission Not needed
Photos & files Choose an existing photo or PDF with Android's system pickers. LienKeeper only receives the file you pick and requests no storage or media permission Not needed
Notifications Local reminders when a lien waiver is still missing after a payment clears, and (Pro) before milestone payments and after the final payment Optional
Run at startup Sets your reminders again after the phone restarts, so they keep arriving. Nothing is sent anywhere Automatic
Internet Google Play Billing for the optional one-time Pro purchase, and Google AdMob ads in the free version. Your records are never sent. Required

LienKeeper does not access your location or contacts, and never records audio. All tracking and reminders work fully offline; without a connection the app simply shows no ads.

💳 Purchases

LienKeeper is free. It offers one optional one-time in-app purchase, “LienKeeper Pro,” which removes all ads and unlocks extra features. There is no subscription.

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see, receive, or store your payment details. Your Pro status is remembered locally on your device and, through Google Play, tied to your Google account so you can restore it after reinstalling.

📣 Advertising

The free version of LienKeeper shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner above the bottom bar on list screens, and occasionally a full-screen ad after you save a new payment or waiver (never on your first day, at most once every three minutes and three times per session). Ads never appear in forms, on a project's money overview, or in notifications.

  • Your records, documents and photos are never shared with AdMob or any advertiser.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in some US states), LienKeeper asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. If you decline, Google shows non-personalized ads. Where offered, you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rated G).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Buying LienKeeper Pro removes all ads.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

📦 Backups, Exports & Sharing

LienKeeper can make a backup file of everything (Pro), a spreadsheet (CSV) export (Pro) and a closeout packet PDF for a title company, lender or buyer (Pro). All of them are under your control:

  • Each file is created on your device. It is only ever shared if you choose to share it or save it somewhere, such as Google Drive.
  • Restoring reads a backup file that you pick. Before replacing anything, LienKeeper keeps a safety copy on your device so you can undo the restore.
  • Nothing is ever sent to us or to any cloud automatically. LienKeeper has no servers to send it to.

🗑️ Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • In the app: Delete payments, waivers, parties, documents or a whole project at any time.
  • Complete removal: Uninstalling LienKeeper permanently deletes all of its data from your device. To remove a copy in your phone's Google backup, manage backups in your Google account.

Because nothing you record ever reaches us, there is no data held on our servers to request deletion of. For ad data held by Google, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or see Google's privacy controls.

⚖️ Not Legal Advice

LienKeeper is a record-keeping tool, not legal advice. It shows what your records hold and what is missing; it does not say whether a waiver is legally valid. Lien laws vary by state; consult a construction attorney for your situation.

👶 Children's Privacy

LienKeeper is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and the ads in the free version are not designed for or targeted at children. Ad content is limited to a G rating.
