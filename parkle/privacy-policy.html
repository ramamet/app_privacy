Parkle Privacy Policy

Car Parking Puzzle

Last updated: 11 October 2026

In short

Parkle has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your progress stays on your phone.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads, and that data contains nothing from your game.

What stays on your phone

Everything Parkle remembers is kept in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your progress: the levels you have cleared and your stars, the daily lots you have cleared and your streak, and your best in Rush
  • Rewards: your coins, the chest's count, the daily gift and the day's missions
  • The Garage and Stats: the paint sets you own and the one you use, and counts such as cars driven out and lots cleared
  • Settings: music, sound effects, vibration and colour shapes
  • Two small things the ads need: how many lots you have cleared since the last full-screen ad, and when it showed, so those ads stay rare

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. The game itself makes no network request of its own, and plays without a network.

Sharing. When you tap Share after a daily lot or a Rush run, the app hands a short text (your result, such as stars and bumps) to your phone's own share sheet. You choose where it goes; the app sends nothing itself and receives nothing back.

Android backup. If Android's backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include the game's progress and settings in it, so they can come back on a new phone. This is Android's own feature, under your control in your phone's settings; we never see the backup.

Advertising

Parkle is free, and is paid for by ads. There are none at all until you have cleared your first 14 levels. After that:

  • A banner sits at the foot of the home screen, the level list, Stats, the Garage and the play screen, below the game's own buttons;
  • a full-screen ad shows now and then when you tap Next level after clearing a lot (or Done after the daily lot): at most one every three lots cleared and three minutes;
  • no ad ever covers a lot while you are playing it, and there are no ads in Rush.

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks only for ads suitable for all audiences (rated G), and passes nothing about your game to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions: for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later in Settings, under "Privacy choices".

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

Purchases

There are none. Parkle has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned in the game, by clearing levels, opening chests, finishing missions and the daily gift, and spent in the game on hints, clearing the holding bay and new paint in the Garage; they cannot be bought with money and have no value outside the game.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored. Settings in the game also has "Start over", which clears your progress
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Children

Parkle is made for teenagers and adults who enjoy a logic puzzle. It is not directed at children under 13, and the app itself collects no personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to content suitable for all audiences.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here with a new date, and a material change will be noted in the app's release notes. If Parkle ever gained a feature that sent more data anywhere, such as cloud saves or leaderboards, it would be described here plainly before the feature shipped.
