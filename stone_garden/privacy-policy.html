Stone Garden Privacy Policy

Stone Garden: Zen Path Puzzle

Last updated: 24 October 2026

In short

Stone Garden has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your progress stays on your phone.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads, and what Google Play handles if you choose to buy Stone Garden Pro. Neither contains anything from your game.

What stays on your phone

Everything Stone Garden remembers is kept in small files in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your progress: the gardens you have raked, and the line in any garden you left half raked
  • Settings: sound, music and vibration, and the temple or open view
  • Small things the game needs: how many hints you hold, and whether you own Stone Garden Pro

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. The game makes no network request of its own, and plays without a network.

No cloud backup. Stone Garden does not take part in Android's backup, so your progress is not copied to your Google account. If you uninstall the app or move to a new phone, your progress starts again (Stone Garden Pro comes back by itself, from Google Play).

Advertising

Stone Garden is free and is paid for by ads. No ad ever appears while you are raking a garden:

  • A banner at the bottom of the Gardens page and a chapter's page, where you choose a garden. Never on the home screen or in a garden
  • A full-screen ad between gardens, when you go on to the next garden after raking one: never before you have raked ten gardens, at most once every third garden after that, and never twice within two minutes
  • An ad you choose to watch: you hold three hints, and raking a new garden earns one back. When they are used, the game may offer a hint for watching a short ad. It is always your choice, and saying no costs nothing

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks only for ads suitable for a general audience (rated G), and passes nothing about your game to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later with "Privacy choices" in Settings.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

Purchases

Stone Garden offers one optional purchase, Stone Garden Pro: a one-time payment that removes all ads and gives unlimited hints. It is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details or your account. The game only asks Google Play, on your device, whether you own Stone Garden Pro, and keeps that one fact in its local file. Every garden is free to play either way.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Children

Stone Garden is made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to those rated for a general audience.

Changes and contact
