Ant Camp Privacy Policy

Ant Camp: Logic Puzzle

Last updated: 18 October 2026

In short

Ant Camp has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your progress stays on your phone.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads, and what Google Play handles if you choose to buy Pro (no ads). Neither contains anything from your game.

What stays on your phone

Everything Ant Camp remembers is kept in a small file in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your progress: the level you have reached, your stars, what you have built in your camp, your hints, your daily box streak and which tips you have seen
  • Settings: music, sounds and vibration
  • Small things the game needs: how many levels you have played and how many since the last ad, so the ad rules below work, and whether you own Pro
  • A short play log: for your last levels, how long each took, how many mistakes and hints, and whether you won. It helps us tune how hard the puzzles are, and it never leaves your phone

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. The game makes no network request of its own, and plays without a network.

No cloud backup. Ant Camp does not take part in Android's backup, so your progress is not copied to your Google account. If you uninstall the app or move to a new phone, your progress starts again (a Pro purchase comes back by itself, from Google Play).

Advertising

Ant Camp is free and is paid for by ads:

  • A banner at the bottom of the play screen, from level 3
  • A full-screen ad between levels, when you tap Next, Try again or Home after a level ends: never in your first five levels, at most once every third level after that, and never twice within two minutes. Never during a level
  • An ad you choose to watch: when you have no free hints left, the game offers a hint for watching an ad, and when you run out of hearts, one more heart (each also for a few of your stars). It is always your choice, and saying no costs nothing

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks only for ads suitable for a general audience (rated G), and passes nothing about your game to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later with "Privacy choices" on the home screen.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

Purchases

Ant Camp offers one optional purchase, Pro: a one-time payment that removes the banner and the ads between levels. It is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details or your account. The game only asks Google Play, on your device, whether you own Pro, and keeps that one fact in its local file. Stars, hints and camp decorations are earned in the game, never sold.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Children

Ant Camp is made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to those rated for a general audience.
