Privacy Policy for MyGoldCalc

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Introduction

MyGoldCalc ("we", "our" or "the app") is a gold price and making charge calculator for Android, published by Futigo. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

In short: we don't ask for or collect your name, phone number, email or any account. Your calculations and settings stay on your phone. The free app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure those ads.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account or sign-up: we never ask for your name, email or phone number
  • No contacts, photos, files or messages
  • No precise (GPS) location
  • Your calculations, the rates you type in and the amounts you check are never sent to us

Information Stored on Your Device

To work quickly and offline, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • The latest gold rates it has downloaded, so it can show them offline
  • Your settings: city, language, default purity and making charge type
  • Rates you enter yourself (for example, your jeweler's rate) and your last-used making charge
  • Simple app state, such as whether you have seen the introduction and when the app was first opened

We cannot see this information. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows full-screen ads, at most a few times per session and only at natural breaks (for example, when you leave a calculator after seeing a result). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version and language)
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location (city or region level)
  • How you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped)

Depending on your consent and region, ads may be personalised based on this information. We request general-audience ads only.

Your choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ad is requested. If you decline, no ads are requested.
  • Change your choice: where this applies, open Settings → Privacy choices in the app at any time.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones). Ads will then not be personalised to you.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How Information Is Used

  • To show current gold rates and work offline
  • To remember your settings and the rates you enter
  • To show ads that keep the app free, as described above

We do not sell your information. We do not share anything with third parties other than the services listed above.

Children

MyGoldCalc is intended for adults buying or valuing gold. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information on your phone stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. We do not keep any personal data on our servers. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can manage it from your Google account and your phone's ad settings.

Security

The app connects to its services over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Because your calculations and settings never leave your phone, they are protected by your phone's own security.

Disclaimer

Gold rates and calculations in the app are estimates for reference only, may vary by jeweler and city, and are not financial advice. Please verify prices with your jeweler before you buy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
