Irumudi Privacy Policy

Ayyappa Mala Tracker

Last updated: 3 October 2026

In short

Irumudi has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your vratham, your lists, your counts and your group stay on your phone.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads in the free version, and that data contains nothing you recorded in the app. Irumudi Pro removes the ads, and with them that data.

What stays on your phone

Everything Irumudi remembers is kept in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your vratham: the day you wore the mala, which pilgrimage it is, which observances you kept on each day, and your past pilgrimages
  • Your lists: the daily observances, the irumudi list and the packing list, with what you ticked and any items you added
  • The saranam count: today's count, your rounds and your total
  • The journey: the trip date, the route, your stops on the way, whether you have booked the virtual queue, and any season date you corrected
  • The name for the card, if you gave one, and the card design you chose
  • Your group, if you started one (see below)
  • Settings: your language, and whether each reminder is on and at what time
  • Pro: a yes-or-no flag that Pro is unlocked
  • In the free version: when the app was first opened, so the ad rules work

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. The app itself makes no network request of its own, and works without a network.

Your group's members

If you organise a group, you may type in the names of the swamis travelling with you, their phone numbers if you choose to, what each has paid, and the group's expenses. This is information about other people, so we say plainly where it goes:

  • It stays on your phone. Irumudi never sends it anywhere and never reads your contacts; you type each number in yourself.
  • It leaves your phone only when you share it: the group notice or the accounts, sent through Android's share sheet to the chat you choose, or a backup file you save.
  • Please add only people who are happy to be in your group's list, and remove anyone who asks. Removing a member removes their payments with them.

Backups

Your backup file. Because there is no account, Irumudi can save everything you recorded, the group included, as one file, so you can move it to a new phone. The file is made only when you tap Save a backup file, and goes only where you send or save it. Restoring a file replaces what is on the phone. Irumudi reads the file you pick through Android's own file picker and needs no storage permission for it.

Your phone's own backup. Irumudi also lets Android's built-in backup include its records, so they can come back when you set up a new phone with your Google account. This backup is made by your phone, stored in your own Google account, and end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock; it is not a server of ours and we cannot read it. You can turn it off for all apps in your phone's settings, under Google, then Backup.

Sharing

When you share the completion card, the group notice or the accounts, Irumudi makes the picture or the text on your phone and opens Android's share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it, for example to a WhatsApp chat. Once you share it, that app's own privacy policy applies. Irumudi does not need permission to read your photos or storage for this.

Reminders

The morning and evening pooja reminders, and the reminder to book the virtual queue, are off until you turn one on. When you do, or when you restore a backup that has one on, Android asks you to allow notifications; if you say no, the reminders stay off. They are scheduled and shown on your phone; nothing is sent to a server. They may appear on your lock screen, depending on your notification settings. After your phone restarts, Irumudi sets them again.

The booking site

Darshan is booked on the official Sabarimala site, not in Irumudi. Tapping Open the booking site opens that site in your browser; what you do there is covered by the site's own privacy policy. Irumudi is an independent app and is not connected with the Travancore Devaswom Board or the temple.

Advertising in the free version

The free version shows an occasional full-screen ad at three natural breaks: when you leave a chapter of the guide, when you leave the irumudi or packing list, and when you leave the calendar. There is never an ad on the prayers, the saranam counter, today's observances, the completion card or the group's accounts. There is never an ad in your first ten minutes with the app, or straight after you share something or open the app from a reminder. Ads are at least five minutes apart, with no more than three each time you use the app.

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks for ads suitable for a general audience (rated G) only, and passes nothing about your vratham, your group or your use of the app to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions: for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later from Settings, under "Privacy choices".

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

While the saranam counter is open, Irumudi keeps your screen on so it does not go dark while you chant. Android allows this without a permission, and the screen behaves normally again as soon as you leave the counter.

Irumudi does not ask for camera, storage, photo library, location, contacts, microphone, phone or SMS permission.

Purchases

Irumudi is free to download, and the vratham itself is never locked: the day count, the observances, the saranam counter, the prayers, the guide, the lists, the reminders and the backup are free. Irumudi Pro is one optional payment, not a subscription. It removes the ads, and gives group organisers a group of any size, each member's share with who owes and who gets back, and two more completion card designs.

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or UPI details, billing address or any part of the transaction. The app keeps only the yes-or-no flag that Pro is unlocked. Google Play's privacy policy covers the payment itself.

Deleting your data

  • Remove a member, a payment, an expense, a list item or a past pilgrimage, or delete the group, and it is gone from your phone
  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored
  • Your phone's own backup keeps a copy of Irumudi's records until it is replaced or you delete it in your Google account's backup settings
  • Backup files, cards and messages you shared stay wherever you sent them until you delete them
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing. Your Pro purchase is recorded by Google Play, so reinstalling does not lose it: use Restore purchase in Settings or on the Pro page.

Children

Irumudi is made for the teenagers and adults who keep the vratham and organise the pilgrimage. It is not directed at children under 13, and the app itself collects no personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here with a new date, and a material change will be noted in the app's release notes. If Irumudi ever gained a feature that sent more data anywhere, it would be described here plainly before the feature shipped.
