Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026  ·  App: MyBuildCost (com.futigo.mybuildcost)  ·  Publisher: Futigo

MyBuildCost is a house-construction expense tracker, developed and published by Futigo (“we”, “us”, “our”). Your construction records stay on your device. We do not collect them, we cannot see them, and the app never sends them to us or to anyone else. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below.

The short version
  • All your expenses, people, budgets and photos are stored only on your phone.
  • MyBuildCost has no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.
  • We (the developer) do not collect, receive, or store any of your data.
  • The free version shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information such as your advertising ID to serve them. Your expenses and other records are never shared with Google or any ad network.
  • MyBuildCost Pro removes all ads; the ad software is then not started at all.
  • The only network features are ads and in-app purchase, both provided by Google.

1. Information you enter

MyBuildCost lets you record information to manage your construction spending, such as:

  • Expense amounts, dates, notes, categories, tags and construction phases;
  • Receiver details you choose to add (name, and optionally a phone number or UPI ID);
  • Budgets, milestones and rate-card figures;
  • Receipt or progress photos you optionally attach.

This information is saved in a private database on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. We have no access to it.

2. Information we collect

None. We do not run any servers for MyBuildCost and do not receive analytics, usage statistics, location, or any personal or financial data from the app. The app contains no third-party analytics or tracking software. The one exception is advertising, provided by Google and described in section 3. Information collected for ads goes to Google, not to us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of MyBuildCost shows occasional full-screen ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads appear only at natural pauses, such as after you finish recording an expense. They never appear in the middle of entering data, and never in your first few minutes with the app.

To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and send to Google:

  • Your device’s advertising ID;
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them;
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information about the ad software.

What is never shared: your expenses, amounts, receivers, budgets, notes, photos or any other information you enter in MyBuildCost. The ad software has no access to the app’s database.

Your choices:

  • Consent. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent message before any ad is requested. If you decline personalised ads, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use limited data. You can change your choice at any time from More → Preferences → Privacy choices; this option appears only where it applies.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads on some phones).
  • No ads at all. Buying MyBuildCost Pro removes ads completely. For Pro users the ad software is not started and makes no ad requests.

Ads are restricted to content rated suitable for general audiences. Google’s use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The app also keeps, on your device only, the date it was first opened and how many ads have been shown in the current session, so that it can limit how often ads appear.

4. Device permissions

MyBuildCost only requests permissions when you use the related feature:

  • Camera / Photos — only if you choose to attach a receipt or progress photo. Selected images are stored locally with your expense. The app does not upload them.

5. Purchases (MyBuildCost Pro)

The optional one-time MyBuildCost Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play Billing. Your payment is processed entirely by Google — the app never sees or stores your payment-card or billing details. Google’s handling of that transaction is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The app only stores, locally, a simple flag recording whether Pro has been unlocked. Pro also removes all ads.

6. Backup, export & sharing

You can export your data to a backup file (JSON) from within the app. This file is created on your device. If you then choose to share that file — or a report — the data goes only to the destination you pick (for example another app, email, or storage location) through your device’s standard share sheet. MyBuildCost does not send your backups anywhere on its own, and there is no automatic cloud backup.

7. Data security

Because your records never leave your device through the app, they are protected by your device’s own security (screen lock, encryption, and app sandboxing). We recommend keeping a device lock enabled and storing any exported backup files somewhere safe, as those files contain your records in readable form.

8. Data retention & deletion

Your data remains on your device until you remove it. You can:

  • Delete individual expenses, receivers, budgets or milestones within the app;
  • Use Clear All Data in the app to erase everything; or
  • Uninstall the app, which removes its local database and attached photos.

Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. For information Google holds about ads, see Google’s privacy controls at myaccount.google.com and the advertising ID settings described in section 3.

9. Children’s privacy

MyBuildCost is a general-purpose finance/utility tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, and the app’s ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

10. Third-party services

The only third party involved is Google:

  • Google Play, for app distribution and, if you buy Pro, billing;
  • Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform, for ads and ad consent in the free version (section 3).

No other third-party services, SDKs, ad networks, or analytics providers are used.
