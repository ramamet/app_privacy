Rangoli at Home Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short

Rangoli at Home has no account, no sign-up and no server. It uses your camera to show a rangoli on your floor, and nothing you see through the camera is saved or sent by the app.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's services use: the advertising SDK in the free version, and Google Play Services for AR while the camera view is open.

The camera

Rangoli at Home asks for the camera so it can show a rangoli on your real floor. The picture is used live on your phone for as long as the camera view is open. The app does not record it, keep it or send it anywhere.

A photo is only made when you tap Save or Share. If you refuse the camera, the app still opens and you can browse designs, but the floor view will not work.

Google Play Services for AR

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

What stays on your phone

Everything Rangoli at Home remembers is kept in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your liked designs
  • Your line colour for your floor
  • Whether you have seen the first screen
  • Pro: a yes-or-no flag that Pro is unlocked
  • When the app was first opened, so the ad rules work
  • Pictures of designs, kept in the app's cache so they appear quickly; your phone may clear them at any time
  • A note of errors: if something goes wrong, a short technical note is kept on the phone. It is never sent

The designs ship with the app or are drawn on your phone, so they need no internet.

Your phone's own backup

Android's built-in backup may include these files, so your settings can come back on a new phone with your Google account. This backup is made by your phone and stored in your own Google account; it is not a server of ours. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Saving and sharing a photo

When you tap Save, your photo goes to your phone's gallery, in a folder called Rangoli at Home. When you tap Share, the app writes the photo to a temporary file and opens Android's share sheet, and it goes only where you choose to send it. Once you share it, that app's own privacy policy applies. Photos in the free version carry the app's name in a corner. Your photo may show your home, so please choose who you share it with.

Advertising in the free version

The free version shows an occasional full-screen ad after you leave the camera view, if you spent at least 20 seconds there. There is never an ad while the camera view is open, in your first two minutes with the app, or after you saved or shared a photo. Ads are at least three minutes apart, with no more than four each time you use the app.

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks for ads suitable for a general audience (rated G) only, and passes nothing about your floor or your photos to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions: for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your choice through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change it in Settings, under Privacy options.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Privacy, then Ads. Rangoli at Home Pro removes the ads, and with them this data collection.

Permissions

  • Camera: to show a rangoli on your floor
  • Internet and network state: used by ads and Google Play Billing; the app makes no network request of its own, apart from opening this page if you tap its link
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads SDK
  • Billing: to buy Pro through Google Play
  • Wake lock and foreground service: added by the ads SDK

The app does not ask to read your photos, contacts or precise location.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app keeps only a note that Pro is unlocked.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it keeps. Photos you saved stay in your gallery until you delete them. We hold nothing about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

Rangoli at Home is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
