MyopiaKeeper Privacy Policy

MyopiaKeeper: Child Eye Care, by Futigo

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short

MyopiaKeeper keeps your child's treatment records only on your phone. There is no account and no analytics. We (Futigo) never receive, see or store any of your data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses information about your device (never your child's records); MyopiaKeeper Pro has no ads.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Children's names or nicknames, optional birth year and month, clinic and eye doctor names, and the next check-up date.
  • Treatments and the daily log: done or missed, times, hours worn, reasons, who did it, comfort and notes.
  • Check-up results you copy from reports (prescription and axial length), and photos of reports you choose to add.
  • Symptoms and notes, with optional photos.
  • Supplies, replacement dates and optional costs.
  • App settings such as reminder times and theme.

None of this leaves your phone unless you choose to share or export it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no server, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting service that sends data. We can't see your records, so we can't share or sell them.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows a small banner on some list screens and, occasionally, a full-screen ad after you save a new check-up, a new supply or a treatment plan. Ads never appear on the Today screen, the sticker chart or in forms. They come from the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob).

  • What Google may collect: device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address and the approximate location it implies, how you interact with ads, and diagnostics. Google uses these to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Never personalised: MyopiaKeeper requests only non-personalised ads, limited to content suitable for all ages. Your child's names, treatments, logs, measurements, photos and notes are never given to AdMob or anyone else.
  • Your choice: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), you are asked for consent before any ad is requested, and you can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • No ads with Pro: after you buy MyopiaKeeper Pro, the app makes no ad requests.

Report scanning

If you use "Scan the report", text recognition runs on your phone using Google ML Kit's on-device model. The photo is not uploaded for this.

Files you share

You can create a doctor-visit PDF, a CSV export or a backup file. These are made on your phone and only go where you send them (for example, an email to your eye doctor or a folder you choose). Once shared, the recipient's own policies apply.

Purchases

MyopiaKeeper Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We only learn, on your device, whether Pro is unlocked.

Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders for treatments, supplies and check-ups.
  • Run at startup: restores reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Alarms and reminders (optional): only if you turn on on-the-minute bedtime reminders.
  • Biometric: only if you turn on app lock; the unlock happens in Android itself.
  • Photos and camera: through the system picker, only when you add a photo.
  • Internet: used by Google Play Billing and, in the free version, by Google AdMob to load ads. Your records are never sent.

Children

The app is meant for parents and guardians who keep records about their children. It is not designed for children to use alone. Your child's records stay on the parent's device and never reach us or any advertiser; ads in the free version are non-personalised and limited to content suitable for all ages.

Deleting your data

Delete a child in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. That removes the data completely: there is no other copy except backup files you made yourself. Android cloud backup is turned off for this app.

Not medical advice

MyopiaKeeper is a logging and reminder tool. It is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose, recommend treatment or predict results. Always follow your eye doctor's instructions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Contact Futigo through futigo.io.
