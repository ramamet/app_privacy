GoldKeeper Privacy Policy

A private register of your family's gold

Last updated: 25 September 2026

In short

GoldKeeper has no account, no sign-up and no server that holds your register. Every piece you record, its photos, your family members, your pledges and your goals stay on your phone.

We never receive your register. The only data that leaves the device about you is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads in the free version. GoldKeeper Pro removes the ads, and with them that data.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is kept in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your register: each piece's name, type, purity, weights, HUID, purchase date, shop, price paid, notes, owner, who it is promised to, and where it is kept (for example a bank and branch for a locker)
  • Photos of pieces and bills that you take or pick
  • Family members you add, and their gold goals
  • The ledger: gifts, sales, moves, lending, and pledges with the lender, amount, interest rate and due date
  • Gold rates the app has fetched or you have entered
  • Settings: theme, language, weight unit, hide values, automatic rate updates, pledge reminders, a yes-or-no flag that Pro is unlocked, and, in the free version, when the app was first opened and when an ad was last shown (so ads stay within their limits)

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Android's automatic backup is switched off for GoldKeeper, so none of it is copied to your Google account either.

Today's gold rate

When "Update rate automatically" is on (it is by default), the app asks our rate service for the day's international gold spot price in rupees, at launch and when you pull to refresh. The request contains no information about you or your register: only the app's public key and how many days of rates to return. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the service; we do not use it to identify you. Turn the setting off in Settings to stop these requests and enter rates yourself.

Files you create

A backup, a valuation report (PDF) or an Excel export is created on your phone and goes only where you choose: a folder you pick, or an app you share it with, such as WhatsApp, email or Google Drive. Once you share a file, that app's own privacy policy applies to it. A backup contains your whole register and its photos, so keep it somewhere safe.

Pledge reminders

If you use pledge reminders (Pro), the app schedules notifications on your phone for three days before and on a pledge's due date. They are created and shown on the device; nothing is sent to a server. A reminder shows the piece's name and the lender, and may appear on your lock screen depending on your notification settings. Turn reminders off in Settings.

Advertising in the free version

The free version shows occasional full-screen ads at natural pauses, for example after you save a piece or close a tool, served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads never appear while you fill in a form, during a backup or restore, or in the first hour after you install the app.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions: for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

The ads SDK does not receive anything from your register. This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later from Settings, under "Ad privacy choices".

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

GoldKeeper does not ask for camera, storage, photo library, location, contacts, microphone, phone or SMS permission. When you add a photo, Android's own photo picker or camera app hands the app only the picture you choose.

Not financial advice

GoldKeeper is a record-keeping app. It is not a bank, lender, jeweller or adviser. It does not buy or sell gold, and it does not offer, arrange or recommend loans.

The live rate is the international spot price converted to rupees. It excludes import duty and GST, so it is lower than jewellers' rates. Values, gains, exchange figures and loan estimates are indicative only; a jeweller, bank or lender may offer a different amount. Please rely on them for your own records, not for decisions without checking with the jeweller or lender concerned.

Purchases

GoldKeeper is free to download. GoldKeeper Pro is one optional payment, not a subscription. It removes the ads and unlocks unlimited pieces and members, more photos, reports, backup and the pledged gold tracker.

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or UPI details, billing address or any part of the transaction. The app keeps only the yes-or-no flag that Pro is unlocked. Google Play's privacy policy covers the payment itself.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a piece, a ledger entry, a member or a goal in the app, and it is gone from your phone, photos included
  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored
  • Backup, PDF and Excel files you saved elsewhere stay there until you delete them
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing. Your Pro purchase is recorded by Google Play, so reinstalling does not lose it: use Restore purchase on the Pro page.

Children

GoldKeeper is intended for adults managing their family's gold. It is not directed at children, and the app itself collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here with a new date, and a material change will be noted in the app's release notes. If GoldKeeper ever gained a feature that sent more data anywhere, it would be described here plainly before the feature shipped.
