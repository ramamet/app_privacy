Strike Lab Privacy Policy

An offline laboratory for Indian options strategies

Last updated: 14 September 2026

In short

Strike Lab has no account, no server, and no copy of anything you build. The positions you create, the numbers you type and the strategies you open stay on your phone.

The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads in the free version. Strike Lab Pro removes the ads, and with them that data.

What the app itself does not collect

The app's own code makes no network requests. We do not collect, receive or store:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account details
  • The positions you build, the strikes, premiums or lots you enter, or the strategies you open
  • Anything about your real trades, holdings, broker or bank
  • Your precise location, contacts, photos, files or messages
  • Usage analytics or crash reports of our own

There is no analytics tool of ours in the app, and no market data is fetched. Every number in Strike Lab is illustrative, entered by you or taken from a built-in preset.

Advertising in the free version

The free version shows full-screen ads between pages, served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads never appear inside the simulator itself, on the welcome screens, on the STT module, or on the page about what happens to most traders, and never while your finger is on a control.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions: for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice later from Settings.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads. Deleting it stops personalised ads based on that ID across apps.

We ask AdMob not to show ads from the finance category, so the app does not serve trading tips between its pages.

What is stored on your phone

A handful of small settings are saved in the app's private storage so the app remembers them:

  • Your appearance, colour-blind palette, language, default contract and interest rate
  • Whether you have seen the welcome screens and first-use hints
  • A count of how many times the app has been opened, used only so the first session never shows an ad
  • A yes-or-no flag recording that Pro is unlocked

Positions are not saved. Closing the app clears them.

Android's automatic backup is not switched off for Strike Lab, so if device backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may copy these settings to your own Google account. They contain nothing about any position or trade.

Not investment advice

Strike Lab is an educational tool. It is not a trading app, a broker, or an advisor, and it is not registered with SEBI in any capacity.

Every price, premium and payoff in the app is illustrative. Nothing in Strike Lab is a recommendation to buy or sell any security or derivative, and nothing in it is a forecast. The figures on trader outcomes are drawn from SEBI's own published studies and are cited as such.

Options are risky, and most individual traders in this segment lose money. Please consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser before making financial decisions.

Purchases

Strike Lab is free to download. Strike Lab Pro is one optional payment, not a subscription. It removes the ads, opens the full strategy library and adds side-by-side comparison.

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card details, UPI details, billing address or any part of the transaction. The app keeps only the yes-or-no flag above.

Google Play's privacy policy covers the payment itself.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app to remove every setting it stored
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data
  • Buy Pro to stop the ads SDK collecting anything from then on

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing. Your Pro purchase is recorded by Google Play, so reinstalling does not lose it: use Restore purchase in Settings.

Children

Strike Lab is intended for adults. Options are adult financial products, and the app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, and the app itself collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here with a new date, and a material change will be noted in the app's release notes.

If Strike Lab ever gained a feature that sent more data anywhere, it would be described here plainly before the feature shipped.
