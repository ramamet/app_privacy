Hairpin 70 Privacy Policy

Hairpin 70: Mountain Truck Sim

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short

Hairpin 70 has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your progress stays on your phone.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads, and, if you buy Hairpin 70 Pro, the purchase itself, which Google Play handles. Neither contains anything from your game.

What stays on your phone

Everything Hairpin 70 remembers is kept in a small file in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your progress: coins, driving points and your driver level, the lorries, upgrades, paints, back panel words, horns and lights you own and use, the checkpoints you have reached, your best runs, the cargo you picked and how many runs you have driven
  • Daily missions: today's missions and how far along they are
  • Settings: music and sound volume, vibration, which side the throttle is on, graphics quality and language

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. The game makes no network request of its own, and plays without a network.

No cloud backup. Hairpin 70 does not take part in Android's backup, so your progress is not copied to your Google account. If you uninstall the app or move to a new phone, your progress starts again.

Advertising

Hairpin 70 is free and is paid for by ads. There is no banner, and no ad ever appears while you are driving:

  • A full-screen ad between runs, when you leave the results or fail card: never in your first three runs, at most once every second run after that, never twice within 90 seconds, and not right after you chose to watch an ad
  • Ads you choose to watch: after a fail, the game may offer to continue from the last checkpoint you passed for a short ad (once a run; you can also use coins), and the results card may offer to double the run's coins. It is always your choice, and saying no costs nothing

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks only for ads rated for a general audience with parental guidance (PG), and passes nothing about your game to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later with "Privacy choices" in Settings on the home screen.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

Purchases

Hairpin 70 offers one optional purchase, Hairpin 70 Pro, which removes the ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; we never see your payment details. The game keeps only a note on your phone that you own Pro, and asks Google Play again when it starts so a reinstall can restore it. With Pro, the game requests no ads at all. Coins are earned by driving and spent in the garage; they cannot be bought and have no money value.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Children

Hairpin 70 is made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact
