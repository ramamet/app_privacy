Privacy Policy

Function Budget — Every rupee out. Every gift in.

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Function Budget mobile application (package com.futigo.functionbudget) handles your information.

Key Point: Function Budget keeps everything on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your data on any server. There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync. We cannot see what you record, because it never reaches us.

1. Information You Enter

Everything below is entered by you and stored only on your device:

Data Type Description Purpose
Functions Name, occasion type, date, venue, guest count, budget Group everything under one event
Expenses Amount, date, category, sub-event, payment mode, notes Track money spent
Vendors Name, service category, quoted amount, advance, balance, due date Track what is still owed and to whom
People and guests Names, place, relationship, side of the family Identify who gave or received a gift
Gifts received and given Amount or item, gift type, envelope number, sub-event, date The gift register and the reciprocity history
Contributions Contributor, amount, date Track money contributed by family
Preferences Language, theme, reminder settings, Pro status Remember how you like the app set up

1.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

Function Budget does NOT collect, request or access:

  • Personal identification documents (Aadhaar, PAN, government IDs)
  • Bank account numbers, card details or payment credentials
  • Login credentials or passwords — there is no account to log in to
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics, crash telemetry or behavioural data
  • Your device contact list, camera or photo library

Names you enter for guests and vendors are typed by you. The app never reads your phone's contacts.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your entries are used only to run the features you are using:

  • Ledger: record expenses, gifts and contributions
  • Real cost: subtract gifts and contributions from spend
  • Vendor balances: compare advance against quoted amount
  • Reminders: schedule a local notification before a balance falls due
  • Insights: draw charts on your device from your own entries
  • Export: produce a PDF, spreadsheet or backup file when you ask for one

Important: Your data is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling, model training, or any purpose beyond the app's own features. All processing happens on your device.

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

Your data is stored in a SQLite database inside the app's private directory, with preferences in Android's app-private storage. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device unless you export or share it yourself
  • No internet connection is needed to use any feature of the app
  • We have no access to your data and no ability to recover it
  • Android's app sandbox keeps other apps from reading it

3.2 What We Do Not Claim

So that you can judge the risk accurately:

  • The database is not encrypted at rest. It is protected by Android's app sandbox and by your device lock, not by a separate key
  • Backup files are plain JSON and are not encrypted or password-protected
  • The app has no PIN or biometric lock of its own

A gift register names real people and the amounts they gave. Treat an exported file with the same care you would give the paper notebook it replaces.

3.3 Your Responsibility

  • Keep your device secured with a screen lock
  • Back up regularly — if the device is lost, so is the data
  • Store exported registers, reports and backups somewhere you control

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not share, sell, rent or trade your information, because we never receive it. Data leaves your device only when:

  • You share it: exporting a PDF, spreadsheet or backup and sending it through an app you choose. Where it goes then is governed by that app's own privacy policy
  • You restore it: moving a backup file to a new device

Third-Party Services

The app includes no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services. The only third-party service is Google Play Billing, used solely to process the one-time Function Budget Pro purchase and to restore it if you reinstall. Google receives the purchase transaction; it receives none of your ledger data. That exchange is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

This is why the app declares the INTERNET permission. Nothing else in Function Budget makes a network request.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Right How to Exercise
Access your data View everything directly in the app
Export your data Print & share — gift register (PDF), function report (PDF), spreadsheet (XLSX)
Modify your data Edit any function, expense, vendor, person, gift or contribution
Delete your data Delete individual entries in the app, or uninstall to remove everything
Back up and restore Create a JSON backup and restore it on another device
Withdraw consent Uninstall the app. All app data is removed with it

We cannot action a data request on your behalf, because we hold nothing to action. Every control above is in your hands on the device.

6. Permissions

Function Budget declares the following permissions:

The app does not request camera, storage, contacts, location, exact alarms or background service permissions. Decline notifications and every other feature still works — you simply will not be reminded about vendor balances.

7. Purchases

Function Budget Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details at any point. Refunds are handled by Google Play under their refund policy. If you reinstall the app or move to a new device, restoring the purchase is done through your Google account.

8. Information About Other People

A gift register necessarily records other people — guests, relatives and vendors, along with what they gave. That information stays on your device under your control, and you are the one who decides whether to export or share it. Please handle it with the discretion those people would expect.

9. Children's Privacy

Function Budget is a financial application intended for users aged 18 and above. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone — including children — because the app collects nothing.

10. Data Retention

Your data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. When you uninstall Function Budget:

  • The database and all app data in the private directory are deleted by Android
  • Files you exported earlier remain wherever you saved or sent them
  • Backup files you created remain until you delete them yourself

There is no cloud copy. Uninstalling without a backup is permanent.

Summary

In simple terms: Function Budget stores everything on your phone and sends nothing anywhere. There is no account, no cloud and no tracking, and the only network use is the Play Store purchase.
