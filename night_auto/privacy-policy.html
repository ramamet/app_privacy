Night Auto Privacy Policy

Night Auto: Rickshaw Rush

Last updated: 28 October 2026

In short

Night Auto has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your progress stays on your phone.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads. It contains nothing from your game.

What stays on your phone

Everything Night Auto remembers is kept in a small file in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your progress: coins, your best shift, the paints, charms, horns and upgrades you own and use, and how many shifts you have driven
  • Daily missions: today's missions and how far along they are
  • Settings: music and sound volume, graphics quality, on-screen buttons, and the quality your phone was measured at on first launch

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. The game makes no network request of its own, and plays without a network.

No cloud backup. Night Auto does not take part in Android's backup, so your progress is not copied to your Google account. If you uninstall the app or move to a new phone, your progress starts again.

Advertising

Night Auto is free and is paid for by ads. There is no banner, and no ad ever appears while you are driving:

  • A full-screen ad between shifts, when you leave the results card after a shift ends: never in your first three shifts, at most once every second shift after that, never twice within two minutes, and not right after you chose to watch an ad
  • Ads you choose to watch: after your last crash, the game may offer one more life for a short ad (once a shift), and the results card may offer to double the shift's coins. It is always your choice, and saying no costs nothing

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks only for ads rated for a general audience with parental guidance (PG), and passes nothing about your game to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later with "Privacy choices" in Settings on the home screen.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

Purchases

Night Auto has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned by driving and spent in the garage; they have no money value.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Children

Night Auto is made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
