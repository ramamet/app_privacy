TrayKeeper Privacy Policy

Aligner & retainer wear-time tracker

Last updated: October 2026

🔒 Our Commitment

TrayKeeper is built privacy-first. Your treatment is personal, and the data you record about it belongs to you and only you.

TrayKeeper has no account and no servers. Everything you track stays on your device. We do not collect, store, share, or sell your wear data, notes, or photos. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which uses device information described under Advertising below; TrayKeeper Pro removes all ads.

📊 Data Collection

We want to be completely transparent about what is and isn't collected.

  • No sign-up, no account, no profile
  • Your wear data, tray plan, notes and progress photos are never collected and never shared, including with advertisers
  • No usage analytics of our own
  • Advertising (free version only): Google AdMob collects your advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (approximate location) and ad interactions to show and measure ads, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud. The ads SDK may also send Google diagnostic information about how ads load and perform. Google's privacy policy applies to this data.

💾 Data Storage

Everything you create in TrayKeeper is stored locally on your device, in app-private storage that other apps cannot read:

  • Wear & out sessions: Timestamps of when your aligners are in or out, stored in a private on-device database
  • Tray plan & day notes: Your current tray, schedule and notes, stored on device
  • Progress photos: Saved in app-private storage, never uploaded
  • Settings: Your goal, reminders and preferences, stored on device

None of this is ever uploaded to a cloud server or shared with us or any third party, including advertisers.

📱 Permissions

TrayKeeper requests only the minimum permissions needed, and the core tracker works without any of them:

Permission Purpose Type
Camera Take progress photos of your smile. Asked the first time you take a photo; choosing one from your library never needs it Optional
Photo picker Choose an existing photo with Android's system photo picker. TrayKeeper only receives the photo you pick and requests no storage or media permission Not needed
Notifications Local reminders to re-insert your tray and change to the next one Optional
Internet Google Play Billing for the optional one-time Pro purchase, and Google AdMob ads in the free version. Your wear data is never sent. Required
Advertising ID Used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads in the free version. You can reset or delete it in your device settings. Free version

TrayKeeper does not access your location or contacts, and never records audio. All wear-time tracking works fully offline; without a connection the app simply shows no ads.

💳 Purchases

TrayKeeper is free. It offers one optional one-time in-app purchase, “TrayKeeper Pro,” which removes all ads and unlocks extra features. There is no subscription.

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see, receive, or store your payment details. Your Pro status is remembered locally on your device and, through Google Play, tied to your Google account so you can restore it after reinstalling.

📣 Advertising

The free version of TrayKeeper shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the History and Progress screens, and an occasional full-screen ad after you save a past entry (at most once every few hours). Ads never appear on the Home screen, next to your wear timer, or while you switch your trays in or out.

  • Your wear data, notes and photos are never shared with AdMob or any advertiser.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), TrayKeeper asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. If you decline, Google shows non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rated PG or below).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Buying TrayKeeper Pro removes all ads.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

📦 Backup & Restore

TrayKeeper can export your data to a single backup file so you can move to a new phone or keep a copy. This is completely under your control:

  • The backup file is created on your device. It is only ever shared if you choose to share or save it somewhere.
  • Restoring reads a backup file that you pick.
  • Backups are never sent to us or to any cloud automatically. TrayKeeper has no servers to send them to.

🗑️ Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • In the app: Delete individual progress photos, sessions or notes at any time.
  • Complete removal: Uninstalling TrayKeeper permanently deletes all of its data from your device.

Because nothing you record ever leaves your device, there is no data held on our servers to request deletion of. For ad data held by Google, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or see Google's privacy controls.

👶 Children's Privacy

TrayKeeper is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and the ads in the free version are not designed for or targeted at children. Ad content is limited to a PG rating or below.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Changes would reflect updates to app functionality, permissions, or advertising.
