Cube Carriers Privacy Policy

Cube Carriers: Ant Color Flow

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short

Cube Carriers has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your progress stays on your phone.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads, and what Google Play handles if you choose to buy No Ads. Neither contains anything from your game.

What stays on your phone

Everything Cube Carriers remembers is kept in a small file in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your progress: the level you have reached, your stars, your boosters (Undo, +1 Slot, Helper), your daily gift streak and which tips you have seen
  • Settings: music, sounds, vibration and the x2 speed
  • Small things the game needs: how many levels you have played and how many since the last ad, so the ad rules below work, and whether you own No Ads

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. The game makes no network request of its own, and plays without a network.

No cloud backup. Cube Carriers does not take part in Android's backup, so your progress is not copied to your Google account. If you uninstall the app or move to a new phone, your progress starts again (a No Ads purchase comes back by itself, from Google Play).

Advertising

Cube Carriers is free and is paid for by ads:

  • A banner at the bottom of the play screen, from level 3
  • A full-screen ad between levels, when you tap Next, Retry or Home after a level ends: never in your first five levels, at most once every third level after that, and never twice within two minutes. Never during a level

There are no rewarded ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks only for ads suitable for a general audience (rated G), and passes nothing about your game to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later with "Privacy choices" on the home screen.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

Purchases

Cube Carriers offers one optional purchase, No Ads: a one-time payment that removes the banner and the ads between levels. It is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details or your account. The game only asks Google Play, on your device, whether you own No Ads, and keeps that one fact in its local file. Boosters and stars are earned in the game, never sold.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Children

Cube Carriers is made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to those rated for a general audience.

Changes and contact
