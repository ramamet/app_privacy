Koi Garden Privacy Policy

Koi Garden: Stone and Stream

Last updated: 24 October 2026

In short

Koi Garden has no account, no sign-up and no server. Your progress stays on your phone.

We never receive anything about you. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising SDK collects to show ads. It contains nothing from your game.

What stays on your phone

Everything Koi Garden remembers is kept in a small file in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your progress: the gardens you have solved and the garden you are on
  • Settings: sound, music and vibration, and whether you have seen How to play
  • One small count: how many of today's free hints you have used

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. The game makes no network request of its own, and plays without a network.

No cloud backup. Koi Garden does not take part in Android's backup, so your progress is not copied to your Google account. If you uninstall the app or move to a new phone, your progress starts again.

Advertising

Koi Garden is free and is paid for by ads. There is no banner, and no ad ever appears while you are solving a garden:

  • A full-screen ad between gardens, when you tap Next garden after solving one: never before you have solved ten gardens, at most once every third garden after that, and never twice within two minutes
  • An ad you choose to watch: three hints a day are free. After that, the game may offer a hint for watching a short ad. It is always your choice, and saying no costs nothing

Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app asks only for ads suitable for a general audience (rated G), and passes nothing about your game to the ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, for example which ads were shown and whether they were tapped, and app launches
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance information about the ads SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later with "Privacy choices" in Settings on the home screen.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Google, then Ads.

Purchases

Koi Garden has no purchases. Every garden is free to play, and nothing is sold in the game.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings to break the link with past ad data

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Children

Koi Garden is made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to those rated for a general audience.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: contact@futigo.io.
