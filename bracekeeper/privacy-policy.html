BraceKeeper Privacy Policy

BraceKeeper: Back Brace Timer, by Futigo

Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short

BraceKeeper keeps brace-wear records only on your phone, encrypted. There is no account and no analytics. We (Futigo) never receive, see or store any of your data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses information about your device (never your records); BraceKeeper Pro has no ads.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Wearers: a name or nickname, an optional birth year and an optional picture from the app.
  • Braces and the wear plan: brace type and fitting date, daily hours, weaning steps and planned breaks.
  • Wear time: when the brace went on and off, reasons for time out, sick days and edits.
  • Check-ups you copy from clinic letters: Cobb angles, Risser sign, height, notes, and photos of letters or X-rays you choose to add.
  • Skin and comfort: pressure spots on a body map, skin checks and comfort ratings.
  • Back photos you take in the app, exercises (including your own photos or videos from a physio) and exercise sessions.
  • Visits, prep checklists and questions for the clinic.
  • Supplies, replacement dates and optional costs and insurance claims.
  • Optional and private: a daily mood face with a note, and whether periods have started (often asked by the clinic, and only if you turn the question on).
  • Milestones, rewards and app settings such as reminder times and theme.

The database is encrypted with a key kept in Android's secure Keystore. Photos, X-rays and videos are stored separately, encrypted with their own key, and never appear in your gallery. None of this leaves your phone unless you choose to share or export it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no server, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting service that sends data. An error log stays on your phone; it is only sent if you copy it yourself into an email. We can't see your records, so we can't share or sell them.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows a small banner on the History and Visits screens and, occasionally, a full-screen ad after you save a new check-up, visit or supply. Ads never appear on the Today screen, where wear time is logged, or in forms. They come from the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob).

  • What Google may collect: device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address and the approximate location it implies, how you interact with ads, and diagnostics. Google uses these to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Never personalised: because many brace wearers are teenagers, BraceKeeper requests only non-personalised ads, marks every ad request for teen age treatment and limits ads to content suitable for all ages. Names, wear time, check-ups, photos, notes and every other record are never given to AdMob or anyone else.
  • Your choice: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), consent is handled before any ad is requested, and where a choice is offered you can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • No ads with Pro: after you buy BraceKeeper Pro, the app makes no ad requests.

Features that run on your phone

  • Scanning a clinic letter: text recognition runs on your phone using Google ML Kit's on-device model. The photo is not uploaded for this.
  • Read-aloud in the exercise player: uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine.
  • Home-screen widget, Quick Settings tile and notifications: show the wearer's name and wear status where anyone looking at the phone can see them. Reminders can use discreet wording, set in Settings → Reminders.

Files you share

You can create a clinic report PDF, a school letter PDF, a calendar file for a visit, a CSV export or a backup file. These are made on your phone and only go where you send them (for example, an email to your orthotist or a folder you choose). Once shared, the recipient's own policies apply.

Backup files are a plain zip with no password, so you can restore them on a new phone. Keep them somewhere private.

Purchases

BraceKeeper Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We only learn, on your device, whether Pro is unlocked.

Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders when the brace has been off for a while or at bedtime, care routines and upcoming visits.
  • Run at startup: restores reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Alarms and reminders (optional): reminders on the minute; without it they still arrive, a little later.
  • Camera (optional): only when you scan a clinic letter or take a back photo or X-ray photo. Photos never record sound.
  • Photos and files: through the system picker, only when you add a picture, a video or a backup.
  • Internet and advertising ID: used by Google Play Billing and, in the free version, by Google AdMob to load non-personalised ads. Your records are never sent.

Teenagers and children

BraceKeeper is for people who wear a scoliosis brace, most of them teenagers, and for their parents or guardians. It is meant for users aged 13 and over; younger children should use it with a parent. Records stay on the device they were entered on and never reach us or any advertiser, and ads in the free version are non-personalised, treated as for teens and limited to content suitable for all ages.

Deleting your data

Delete a wearer in Settings → Wearers, delete everything at once in Settings → Backup → Delete all data, or uninstall the app. That removes the data completely: there is no other copy except backup files you made yourself. Android cloud backup is turned off for this app.

Not medical advice

BraceKeeper is a logging and reminder tool. It is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose, recommend treatment or predict how a curve will change. Always follow your orthotist's and doctor's instructions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Contact Futigo through futigo.io.
